## **Main Policy 1: Walmart Canada standard return policy**

---

https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/walmart-canada-standard-return-policy/6657a60d5a3f4e6cbd1409330b680562

With a valid proof of purchase, almost anything purchased from Walmart, whether in-store or on Walmart.ca\*, can be returned or exchanged within 90 days,

\* Walmart.ca purchases: The Walmart Canada Standard Return Policy outlined in this document  applies to purchases that are sold and shipped by Walmart, as well as Marketplace items shipped and fulfilled by Walmart. This policy does not apply to purchases that are both sold and shipped by Marketplace sellers; please consult the Marketplace Return Policy.

Oversized items (larger than 40” x 48”) and overweight items (weighing more than 100 lbs), such as full-size mattresses and large appliances, purchased on Walmart.ca cannot be returned in-store and require a scheduled return delivery.

Item exchange eligibility follows this Walmart Canada Standard Return Policy. If an item is not eligible for a return, it will not be eligible for an exchange.

Timeframes:

* Online purchases: the return timeframe begins on the date of your order’s arrival.  
* In store purchases: the return timeframe begins on the purchase date.

During the holidays, the Holiday Return Policy may extend the return periods.

## **Main Policy 2: Holiday return policy**

---

### How long do I have to return an item?

https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/holiday-return-policy/37f4816768034b5daac7e8d056d8271f  
With proof of purchase, any unopened items outside of the 90-day return period that are purchased between November 1 and December 25, the return period begins on December 26\. This excludes items sold and shipped by Marketplace sellers as well as contract post-paid wireless devices. Final sale exceptions still apply.

Items with a return period of 30 days: eligible to return until January 24, 2025\.  
Items with a return period of 14 days: eligible to return until January 8, 2025\.  
Items with a return period of 90 days: extended holiday return policy does not apply.

Please note that return policies for items sold and shipped by [Marketplace sellers](https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/return-policy/5c6aa18cf55d4f13b8753bc0fe343e8a) may differ. Items sold by Marketplace sellers and [fulfilled by Walmart](https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/fulfilled-by-walmart-return-policy/f8640a528dfc4f9c9db2322dcdcb1e1d) follow Walmart Canada’s holiday return policy above. Learn more about our [return policy](https://www.walmart.ca/en/help/article/return-policy/6657a60d5a3f4e6cbd1409330b680562) for online orders, including how to complete a self-service return online.

### I bought an item from a Marketplace seller on Walmart.ca. Can I return it at a Walmart store?

Yes\! For your convenience and peace of mind, eligible Marketplace items can be returned to Walmart stores.  
For Marketplace items related to the categories listed below, contact the seller directly. These items will not be accepted in stores.

* Fuel Powered Equipment  
* Dangerous goods (i.e. hazardous materials containing chemicals, compressed gas or liquids which could pose a health or safety risk, etc)  
* Oversized or overweight products (i.e. weighing greater than 100 lbs or larger than 40” by 48”, such as but not limited to full-size mattresses and large appliance items, etc)  
* Where Items exceed $250 in value  
* Where Items are on Recall  
* Where Items are reported to cause Bodily Injury or Property Damage

Please note, Marketplace items returned to a Walmart store must include all original packaging and materials as well as the order number.

### How do I initiate a return for an item sold by a Marketplace seller?

We’re sorry that something didn’t go right\! If the item is eligible for a return, please follow the steps below to return it:

In store return:

* Please bring the item(s) along with all the original packaging and materials to the Customer Service desk at your Walmart store. We also require your order number to process the return. Please note, only items valued less than $250.00 CAD are eligible for in store returns. If you wish to return items with a retail price above $250.00 CAD, please reach out to the Marketplace seller directly.

Direct to Seller return:

* Click the “Contact Seller” option in "[Purchase History](https://www.walmart.ca/en/orders)" in your account.  
* Select the item(s) you wish to return.  
* Select the reason as “Returns".  
* Please briefly explain the reason why you would like to return the product.  
* Click the “Submit” button.

Please note that customers may be required to pay return shipping charges on direct to seller returns.
